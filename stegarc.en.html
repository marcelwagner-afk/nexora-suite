Competitive position · as of November 2026

Who delivers the one interface for all applications?

Every major vendor declares its assistant or portal to be the single front door. None of them is vendor-neutral while also unifying the actual work: actions, screens and approvals across system boundaries. That is exactly where STEGARC fits: first as a neutral interface over all systems, then an individual ERP dashboard that is expanded process by process until it replaces the legacy systems.

03 · White space

Four areas where STEGARC stands out

Neutral orchestration

Coordinating the assistants instead of competing with them

A group with SAP, Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow will soon have three "single" front doors. STEGARC routes each task to the right agent and shows the result in the same interface.

Finding: Joule, Copilot, Gemini Enterprise, Slack and EmployeeWorks all claim the front-door role. Workday shows with Workday Everywhere that "running inside the others" works.

SAP licence-aware

Compliance by design instead of agent tax as a surprise

For every access to SAP, it is visible which approved route it takes and what it costs: Joule via A2A, MCP Gateway or released interface.

Finding: SAP API Policy v4/2026 restricts external agents (source is an opinion piece, check original text before making customer statements). DSAG: 3 % use Joule in production.

Screens in the dialogue

Tables, approvals and KPIs directly in the conversation

Pure chat fails at structured work. STEGARC shows real screens in the conversation flow and jumps into the full application via deep link when needed.

Finding: MCP Apps has been part of the MCP standard since July 2026 and runs in Claude, ChatGPT and M365 Copilot. Every action is logged as an auditable message.

European & co-determination-ready

Audit-proof, works council-compatible, safe from discontinuation

Every action in the event log, roles and four-eyes principle visible, operation on-premises or hybrid. This addresses data residency and co-determination.

Finding: Viva Goals, Citrix Microapps and Meta Workplace have been discontinued. Customers are looking for stability. The STEGARC engine is already event-based and reproducible.

04 · Risks

Where the positioning is vulnerable

Microsoft's sales channel

Teams and Outlook are the workplace anyway. Another separate interface will struggle to gain acceptance.

Response: STEGARC also runs embedded in Teams and Copilot, not just as a separate page.

Cancellation rate of agentic projects

Gartner expects over 40 % of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027.

Response: start with narrowly defined use cases and measurable benefits, such as approvals across system boundaries.

SAP access

So far, the SAP policy is only documented via an opinion piece and may change.

Response: keep the integration route interchangeable per system and check the original policy text.

05 · From layer to your own ERP

First overlay, then replace process by process

Because every task is already completed in the STEGARC interface, employees do not notice which system is working behind it. This creates an individual ERP dashboard for each company, based on its processes and roles. Behind it, one process after another is moved from the legacy system to the STEGARC engine until the old systems can be switched off. In software modernisation, this approach is called the strangler pattern.
